01

Mark exploration

THREE CONCEPTS · ONE PRIMARY
PRIMARY

Bracket

RISK ENVELOPE

Two brackets holding a position between them, on one unit: stroke 3.6, arm 9, centre 4.4 for optical weight. Bounded, supervised risk stated as a single shape.

ONE DRAWING AT EVERY SIZE

Pillar

T MONOGRAM

An architectural T: chamfered capital, heavy stem, a detached plinth. Reads as foundation and load-bearing strength — but a letter-mark leans on the name rather than the product.

Monolith

TRILITHON

A gate cut from two masses and a lintel — the oldest built form, a titan reduced to pure load path. Imposing at any size, but it speaks about architecture in general rather than about control.

Recommendation — Bracket. It is the only concept that says what Titan does rather than what it is called: a position held inside bounds. One drawing serves every size — no simplified 16 px variant, no hairlines to lose — and it is unmistakably not a candlestick, an arrow or a monogram.

07

Rationale & handoff

TOKENS FOR §7 OF THE REDESIGN SPEC
WHY THE BRACKET

Two brackets and the position they hold. It states supervision instead of spelling the name, carries no hairline or curve to lose, and stays itself at 16 px, in one colour, and on a statement PDF — one drawing, every size.

WHY INDIGO VIOLET

Cyan is the category default; violet is ownable and reads as software craft rather than terminal chrome. It also keeps the maximum hue distance from green and red, so the signature colour can never be misread as direction or profit.
