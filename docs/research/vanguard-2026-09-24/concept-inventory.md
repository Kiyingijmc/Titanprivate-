# E. Adversarial concept inventory

Both requested classification systems are retained. Scientific class describes the mechanism; disposition describes this proposed program. KEEP means keep the requirement or minimal abstraction, **not permission to implement**. TEST means a separately registered experiment; DEFER means absent from V1. A conditional experiment never counts as evidence that a feature works.

For every row: the rationale identifies the hypothesized information or engineering value; the final column identifies the simpler competitor or falsifier. Unless expressly noted, all measurements are causal descriptive price statistics—not demonstrated causal economic drivers. Every stateful TEST requires identical replay/live transitions, availability timestamps and persisted initialization. No row permits future labels or retroactive state changes.

## Collapsed concepts

The minimal evidence record is direction/normalized displacement, volatility and data availability. Efficiency is one optional incremental feature. Health is a view of explicit guards; confidence is omitted unless calibrated; debt is omitted while dwell exists; exit pressure is replaced by the selected action and reason. This removes multiple transformations of the same evidence without losing observability.

Trend, campaign and trade retain separate identities because they answer different accounting questions. Anchor/Satellite remain optional roles because they do not necessarily add predictive information. Structural protection, software invalidation and the current broker stop remain distinct concepts because their failure modes and confirmation requirements differ.
