# M15 SilverBullet sweep/reclaim: session and supplemental-data validation

Follow-up: [frequency and entry experiment](2026-09-17-sb-frequency-entry-results.md)
compares seven frozen arms. A 09:30–11:00 window increases fills while keeping
the midpoint entry; shallow entries and the tested H1 alignment rule disappoint.
None passes every advance criterion.

## Decision

**Remain research-only.** The source-supported seasonal clock materially
improves the later historical fixed-exit result, but the candidate still
fails early sample size, early cost stress and breadth requirements.
The newer supplemental sample contains only two filled trades, both losses.
It is too small to establish either profitability or a reliable rejection.

Rules were frozen before these returns were read:
[session validation design](2026-09-17-sb-session-validation-design.md).
The M15 detector, midpoint entry, structural stop, first daily NY signal,
pending TTL, holding limit, exits and costs are unchanged. No live strategy
or runtime settings were changed.

## Clock finding

The initial screen deliberately used a fixed UTC+2 assumption. FBS documents
GMT+3 from the last Sunday in March and GMT+2 from the last Sunday in October
on its [trading-hours page](https://fbs.com/trading/trading-hours?lang=en).
Its [trading conditions](https://cdn.fbs.com/docs/Trading_conditions_en.pdf)
also describe platform time as generally GMT+2/GMT+3, subject to change.

Local evidence is consistent with summer UTC+3: EURUSD's last warmup candle
is 2.94–2.98 hours ahead of the UTC capture-directory timestamp on July 28,
August 7 and September 16. The gap from exactly three hours is consistent
with the age of the last M5 bar. Capture-directory names are generated by
datetime.utcnow in SystemController._snapshot_warmup.

The seasonal candidate correction therefore uses Europe/Helsinki for broker
wall time and America/New_York for session conversion. This is a documented,
source-supported inference, not complete historical account/server timezone
certification. The historical exporter labels timestamps UTC while the
legacy research treats the files as broker wall time; authoritative raw-epoch
reconciliation remains necessary. No profitable clock was chosen after the run.

Ambiguous/nonexistent broker times are rejected. Tests explicitly cover
the March and October weeks when US and European DST schedules differ.
Session admission is determined at the M15 candle close, not its open.

Seasonal stressed fills are 107 early and 103 late. Later seasonal fixed-exit
profit factor is 1.434 normally and 1.364 under stress. These are modest
historical samples selected after the wider 60-model experiment, not a fresh
holdout or statistical proof of edge.

The seasonal runner's later means are +0.06978/+0.08599R normally and
+0.02159/+0.04364R under stress for O-H-L-C/O-L-H-C. Its early stressed
means are negative (-0.01660/-0.05354R). This experiment does not justify
assuming that the runner improves this reversal-style entry.

**Why there is still no advance:**

- Early seasonal fills (109 normal, 107 stress) miss the declared 150 floor.
- Every clock has negative early fixed-exit mean under cost stress.
- Later seasonal breadth lacks adequate per-symbol samples. Only US30 has
  at least 20 fills (22); the rule requires at least three sufficiently
  sampled symbols before judging majority-positive breadth.
- Seven of nine symbols have positive later seasonal fixed-exit means, but
  most have only 5–18 fills. BTCUSD and XAUUSD are negative. Removing them
  after reading this table would be post-hoc symbol selection.

All 56 clock/period/exit/path/cost cells, including supplemental cells, are
preserved in the [summary CSV](2026-09-17-sb-session-validation-summary.csv).
The first-screen UTC+2 baseline reproduces all 3,336 relevant order rows
within 1e-12 numerical tolerance, including unfilled orders and scenarios.

## Newer local data

Inventory: 173 M5 warmup snapshot files, 86,500 overlapping rows. After
removing forming/ambiguous candles, exact duplicates and conflicting
timestamps, 34,402 unique completed bars remain across nine symbols. The
sample spans July 27–September 16, 2026, with substantial gaps. Nine
symbol/timestamp conflicts were discarded. There are 144 continuous
segments; 110 can supply enough M15 warmup for evaluation.

This is a **limited post-history supplemental sample**, not a continuous
July–September backtest or an independently sourced holdout. It comes from
the operational buffers and overlaps across restarts. Sampled journal ticks
were not used to reconstruct missing execution bars.

Only five eligible candidates remain after conservative segmentation and
session filtering: two fill and three expire. The USDCAD July 31 and US30
September 14 fills both stop out. Their combined result is -2.05229R at
normal costs and -2.07844R under stress. All path/exit scenarios agree for
these two trades. There are no marked filled tails in this sample. Repeating
the two trades across scenarios does not create additional observations.

Insufficient coverage and sample size prevent a meaningful forward-validation
claim. The losses cannot be used to estimate a stable live loss rate.

## Implementation and verification

- `src/research/sb_sessions.py`: explicit clock conversion, first-per-day
  admission across segments, completed-snapshot assembly, conflict rejection
  and splitting at missing M5 intervals.
- `scripts/sb_session_validation.py`: all three historical clocks plus the

  source/data hashes and complete result exports.

  timing, daily deduplication, forming candles, conflicts, gaps and invalid data.

## Next requirement

Keep the seasonal-clock M15 fixed-exit version as a hypothesis to observe,
not a deployable winner. Collect a continuous, independently time-verified
M5/tick history with bid/ask information and extend the sample before changing
parameters. Broker confirmation, slippage, variable spread, lot rounding,
news and portfolio constraints remain outside this idealized replay.
No lowering of the original gates follows from the stronger later-period cell.
