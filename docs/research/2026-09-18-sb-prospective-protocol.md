# Frozen prospective quote-sampled shadow protocol

Two fixed candidates: `wider_midpoint` (M15 sweep/reclaim, midpoint LIMIT, New York parent closes 09:30–11:00) and `session_range` (the frozen completed-session-range parent from September 18). Import the existing detector implementations unchanged. Nine symbols: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, GBPJPY, XAUUSD, US30 and BTCUSD. No optimization or symbol exclusion during collection.

A new isolated SQLite cohort starts at its recorded UTC creation time. Historical warmup bars are allowed only as context. No pre-cohort entry may be backfilled. Signals are recognized only on completed bars, discovered within 90 seconds of the parent close, and submitted hypothetically at observation time, never at an earlier favorable quote. The detector's first eligible parent/day rule continues to count earlier parents even if the recorder started later. Repeated polling and restarts must not duplicate decisions.

The bridge timestamp labels currently encode broker-local wall time. Convert using seasonal Europe/Helsinki to UTC, then America/New_York for strategy sessions. Store the original tick label and converted timestamp. Reject quotes over 15 seconds old or more than two seconds ahead of receipt. Record and exclude malformed, out-of-order and wrong-clock quotes. All prices must be finite and bid positive with ask >= bid. Quote time, receipt time and raw bid/ask are preserved.

Poll current quotes every two seconds and fetch closed-bar context every 30 seconds. These are sampled observations, not a complete tick stream. No OHLC-based filling or interpolation between observations is allowed. A gap exceeding 15 seconds in valid observations censors pending and open hypothetical orders before processing the returning quote; stopped recording also censors active orders. Censored trades have no performance result and do not count toward review thresholds. Restarts retain the original cohort and immutable policy, verify source/spec hashes and recover existing state conservatively. Warmup inputs and original observations are never rewritten if broker history changes.

Each recognized setup has two execution scenarios: observed bid/ask with modeled commission, and 1.5x observed spread plus 1.5x commission. The latter holds bid fixed and widens ask. The normal model-cost gate used by the frozen detector remains unchanged; each execution scenario also rejects a submission whose observed spread plus modeled commission exceeds 0.25 original R. Rejected submissions consume that parent; there is no alternative-parent search or market fallback. This additional observed-quote check and actual observation latency make prospective execution distinct from historical idealized replay.

BUY limits fill when an eligible observed ask <= entry; SELL limits fill on bid >= entry. Hypothetical fill price is the requested limit, with no favorable gap improvement. Use only quotes with market timestamps at or after admission. Long exits use observed bid and short exits observed/scenario ask. Preserve the planned absolute stop and 2R target. Adverse opening/observation gaps stop at the executable quote, including the same quote that fills a pending limit. Favorable target gaps are capped at the target. Pending TTL remains 45 minutes after parent close (exclusive). Hold for at most 180 minutes after observed fill, then close on the next valid observed quote, with stop/target precedence. Sampled quotes can miss fills or stop/target crossings even during nominally healthy coverage; label results accordingly.

Commission is the existing modeled $7 round-turn/lot assumption converted using frozen tick_size/tick_value, charged once per completed initial risk unit. Do not claim these are actual broker fees or portfolio returns. Store entry, risk, stop, target, fill/exit quote IDs, prices, timestamps and net R. Retain raw quotes, completed bars, reason-coded decisions, errors and a hash of each available bar context.

Review only after each candidate's two scenarios independently have >=100 complete, uncensored trades, >=3 symbols with >=20 such trades, a majority of those symbols positive, and positive aggregate mean net R. A ready flag requests human research review, never live deployment. This is a separate prospective gate, not a claim that the failed historical early/late gates have been met. Show collection progress and incomplete observations continuously, but do not retune rules in response to interim returns. Two overlapping candidates and two execution scenarios are not independent samples.

The runner uses a dedicated GET-only transport that rejects broker writes, a separate database/directory, and a localhost-only dashboard exposing read/export endpoints. It does not load the trading controller, use the live trade-state database, or expose trading commands. Credentials stay in the environment and are excluded from the cohort manifest and error logs.

Storage uses SQLite WAL with NORMAL synchronization to keep synchronous disk flushes out of every quote transaction. Committed state survives ordinary process restarts; sudden host power loss can lose recent uncheckpointed observations. Treat host-power-loss recovery as a data-integrity review, not proof of uninterrupted coverage. SQLite consistency does not establish that every market observation was persisted. Graceful shutdown checkpoints the database after censoring active orders.
