TURN 1 · SYMBOL RESTART

Six new marks for Titan

The gyroscope is retired. These go wider — including two the original brief ruled out. Every one is drawn from primitives only, holds at 16 px, and works as a solid silhouette in one colour. The wordmark is unchanged. Reply with an id (1c) and I'll develop it.

92PX · 16PX · MONO · LOCKUP SWITCH STAGE TO LIGHT IN TWEAKS
1a

Keystone

ARCHITECTURE

The wedge that locks an arch, floating above the two piers it binds. Pure load path, no metaphor to decode — and the gap gives it tension the other solid marks don't have.

1b

Aperture

INSTRUMENT

Four blades closing on a square opening — the exposure control of an optical instrument. Says "calibrated" without a dial, and the pinwheel offset makes it unmistakable.

1c

Bracket

RISK ENVELOPE

Two brackets holding a position between them. The most literal statement of what Titan sells — bounded, supervised risk — and the simplest shape here at 16 px.

1d

Ziggurat

MASS

A stepped monument, widest at the base. Titan as accumulated scale and stability. Reads as a monument rather than a chart because it narrows upward and never varies its bar heights.

1e

Ringed body

SATURN VI

Titan is Saturn's largest moon — a body held in a ring system. The only concept with a real story behind the name, and the ring's shallow angle keeps it a planet, not a prohibition sign.

1f

Cut T

NEGATIVE SPACE

The brief ruled out a monogram — but as a void cut from a solid tile it stops being a letter and becomes an object. Born as an app icon; the strongest of the six at 16 px.

My read: 1c and 1f are the two that survive every size without help — 1c is the smarter idea, 1f is the better app icon. 1e is the one with a story worth telling in an investor room.
